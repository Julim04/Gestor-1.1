## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-tier, serene, and functional operational dashboard crafted specifically for managing rural retreats, estates, and vacation farmsteads. Blending modern utility with refined hospitality aesthetics, the interface communicates calm efficiency, natural prestige, and absolute operational clarity.

The visual style merges **Minimalist Precision** with subtle **Glassmorphic Tonal Layering**. It delivers an immersive, dark-slate atmosphere energized by organic emeralds and glowing mint accents. The experience avoids chaotic clutter, relying instead on deliberate negative space, razor-sharp typographic scale, and luminous micro-interactions that evoke peaceful outdoor sanctuaries without compromising data density.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep graphite bedrock offset by luminous botanic greens. High-contrast legibility is maintained across all operational states.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - `Base Floor`: `#0B1117` — Deepest graphite backdrop.
  - `Surface Level 1` (Cards & Panels): `#161F2E` — Primary elevation.
  - `Surface Level 2` (Floating Controls, Hover States): `#1E293B` — Higher component tiers.
  - `Surface Borders`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `#334155` for crisp structural outlines.
- **Accents & Brand Identifiers**:
  - `Emerald Primary`: `#10B981` (Base), `#059669` (Hover), `#047857` (Active/Deep).
  - `Mint Radiance`: `#34D399` (Vibrant accent), `#6EE7B7` (Luminescent highlights), `#A7F3D0` (Subtle text on green badges).
- **Functional Semantics (Status Badges)**:
  - `Confirmed`: `#10B981` text over `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` background, emerald border glow.
  - `Pending`: `#F59E0B` text over `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` background.
  - `Cancelled`: `#F43F5E` text over `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)` background.
  - `Completed`: `#94A3B8` text over `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)` background.
- **Text & Content**:
  - High Emphasis: `#F8FAFC`
  - Medium Emphasis: `#94A3B8`
  - Muted/Disabled: `#475569`

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric precision paired with friendly, open apertures. 

Headings prioritize tight kerning (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create a polished architectural hierarchy for financial numbers, property titles, and administrative metrics. Small labels and data indicators utilize uppercase or semi-bold weights with slight positive tracking to ensure absolute readability in dark environments across all monitor calibrations.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid orchestrates wide desktop dashboards, shifting down to a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile`: `< 768px` (Fluid 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, gutters condensed).
  - `Tablet`: `768px - 1199px` (Fluid 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, side navigation collapses into rail).
  - `Desktop`: `>= 1200px` (Fluid 12 columns, max content cap at `1600px`, fixed vertical admin sidebar of `280px`).

The layout rhythm follows an 8pt spatial grid. Component interior paddings prioritize breathability (`space-lg` inside main stat cards) to prevent cognitive fatigue during extended operational work.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through **Tonal Layering** complemented by ambient, emerald-tinted glows rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Surface Level 0 (Ground)**: `#0B1117` base floor with no elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Structural Containers)**: `#161F2E` with a hairline stroke `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` and subtle ambient shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns)**: `#1E293B` backed by a 1px border `rgba(110, 231, 183, 0.15)` and soft depth `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Emerald/Mint Glow Effect**: Key callouts, selected dates on availability calendars, and primary interaction targets utilize an atmospheric glow: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Glass Accents**: Sticky navigation bars and floating filter bars employ `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces generous, organic contours that reflect leisure retreat architecture:

- **Base Elements (`roundedness: 2`)**: Buttons, input controls, chips, and small cards adopt a standard `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) radius.
- **Primary Cards & Containers**: Use `rounded-xl` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for expansive analytics cards and property overview modules.
- **Badges & Avatars**: Utilize full pill geometry (`9999px`) to maintain soft contrast against sharp analytical data.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Emerald (`#10B981`) text in pure white or deepest green (`#042F2E`), with hover shift to `#059669` and a soft mint glow. 10px 20px padding, `rounded-xl`, semi-bold weight.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.08)` and border to `#34D399`.

### Status Badges (Chips)
- Height: 28px, shape `rounded-full`, inline padding 12px. Font: `label-sm` with `0.04em` tracking.
- Subtle tinted borders matching status colors at 30% opacity. A miniature 6px pulsing dot precedes the label text for live/active states.

### Input Fields & Search
- Surface: `#0F172A` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Height: 44px, `rounded-xl`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#10B981`, supported by an outer ring glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`. Placeholder rendered in `#475569`.

### Cards & Estate Modules
- Composed of `#161F2E` with interior padding `space-lg`.
- Top header featuring estate thumbnail with `rounded-lg`, property name in `title-sm`, accompanied by current occupancy status indicator.

### Calendar & Date Range Pickers (Booking Specific)
- Selected ranges render with continuous horizontal fill `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.16)`.
- Check-in and check-out anchor dates rendered in solid `#10B981` with white high-contrast text and mint box shadow.
- Blocked/Maintenance dates styled with diagonal subtle hash pattern in `#334155`.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- Checkbox: 20x20px square with 6px border radius. Unchecked: `border: 1.5px solid #475569`. Checked: background `#10B981` featuring a crisp white SVG check.
- Toggle Switch: Track in `#1E293B`, thumb in white, active track shifts smoothly to linear gradient of `#10B981` to `#34D399`.
